Cunningham source-faithfulness audit
===================================
Completed 2026-11-02. This is the source review requested after discussion
of the three boundary reward coefficients.

Conclusion
----------
The three coefficients retained in the mission are required by the paper's
graph and outgoing-edge reward convention. For each of b_2^0,c_2^0,d_2^0,
eliminating g_1,F_1,h_1 contributes G_1+epsilon*H_1 and sends epsilon flow
to a_2 and 1-epsilon flow to e_1. This is a general algebraic derivation,
not an adjustment selected to obtain the expected pivot counts.

The source-level audit and independent semantic/code review are complete.
The package is a reviewed rational reconstruction of the graph-derived
construction, with the source conventions below made explicit. This does
not mean its nine research theorem targets have been proved.

--------

   arbitrary n>=3, deriving the entire collapsed stochastic model and LP
   from Figures 7 and 8. It checks rewards, probabilities, all boundary
   cases, source parameters, and the initial-state discrepancy.

   The central bottom_graph_matches_boundary_action theorem connects the

Source conventions that remain explicit
---------------------------------------
The paper's displayed Section 4.4 objective uses priority notation where
the preceding stochastic construction uses priority-induced rewards. Even
after that interpretation it lacks the three alias contributions. The
mission follows the graph and general translation, not that display alone.
The F_1 transition follows the boundary drawing and its conservation row.
Sections 2.3 and 4.4 explicitly give the chosen e_1^1 start; Sections 3.3 and
5.2 use conflicting all-e-zero shorthand. The companion lower bounds use
the full counter-run transfer, not subtraction of one from a bound of 2^n.
No author confirmation or published erratum has been established.

Uniform indexing check
----------------------
For n>=3 the five row blocks have lengths n-1,n-2,n-1,n-1,n, with
starting offsets 0,n-1,2n-3,3n-4,4n-5. Their total is 5n-5. These are
exactly the branches of rowName; rowRank subtracts the branch's starting
level and adds the corresponding offset. Consequently they name each
source decision vertex once, with no b_n and no a_1,c_1,d_1 decision row.

The first column group contains e_1^1, then n-2 complete four-action
blocks, then d_n^0,e_n^1: 1+4(n-2)+2=4n-5 columns. The remaining groups
have lengths 2(n-1),n,n-1,2(n-1), so their successive starting offsets are
4n-5,6n-7,7n-7,8n-8 and the total is 10n-10. These match columnName's
branches. Quotient/remainder modulo 4 or 2 picks the level and action in
the respective block; columnRank reverses those operations. Each valid
rank lies in [0,10n-10), so the modulo in actionColumn and initialBasis
does not change it. This gives an arbitrary-n arithmetic check of the
naming/order interface, separately from the finite exported-data checks.

Limits of the completed review
------------------------------
Source-to-Lean faithfulness necessarily includes mathematical reading of
the paper. The new kernel certificates prove the disputed local algebra
and its code connection; they do not formalize the entire arbitrary-n
graph-to-LP equivalence. The all-action derivation and code inspection are
recorded mathematical review, supplemented by exact finite comparisons.
The rational-to-real polytope argument is supplied in the semantic audit,
but is not a separate Lean transport theorem. All nine exponential-bound
and companion research targets remain open. The platform's human proposal
self-audit and submission have not been performed by this review.
