P1 — Corollary 4.4 counts only outer list entries and is false for nested data.
Affected item: AehligSchwichtenberg2002.corollary_4_4, theorem ID ee8fa0d7-cd23-4f23-8a0e-f01805651c6e.
Location: code/06_AehligSchwichtenberg2002_corollary_4_4.lean:4-9.
The statement quantifies over every Ty τ and bounds output listEntryCount by input listEntryCount. Take τ=L(B). A closed affine program of type L(L(B)) ⊸ L(L(B)) can turn a singleton outer list containing a singleton inner Boolean list into a two-element outer list of empty inner lists. The two diamond tokens are preserved: one came from the outer cons and one from the inner cons. The outer list length therefore increases from 1 to 2 despite no increase in resource use.

Explicit program, using the source's notation:
  h = λ e^⋄, b^B, q^{L(L(B))}. cons_{L(B)} e nil_B q
  g = λ d^⋄, a^{L(B)}, z^{L(L(B))}.
        cons_{L(B)} d nil_B (a {h} z)
  f = λ xs^{L(L(B))}. xs {g} nil_{L(B)}
  input = cons_{L(B)} d (cons_B e tt nil_B) nil_{L(B)}
  output = cons_{L(B)} d nil_B (cons_{L(B)} e nil_B nil_{L(B)})
Here d and e are distinct free diamond variables, permitted by IsCanonicalListInput. The input and output are normal, and f input reduces to output in eleven steps.

Evidence: counterexample.lean imports only the submitted definition modules and constructs the typing derivation, the normal input, all eleven reductions, normal output, and the contradiction to m≤1. build_counterexample.py is the generator; the Lean kernel is the checker. The certificate compiles successfully in Lean 4.33.1; counterexample-check.log records failed 0 and axiom dependencies restricted to propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, with no sorryAx. The theorem submitted_corollary_4_4_false refutes the full submitted universal statement, with all four hypotheses supplied.

Source comparison: the author preprint https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/0011037 (v2), Corollary 4.4 on PDF p.11, prefaces the claim as about type-1 functions. Its proof relates list length to free-variable resource count. The submitted universal τ and unrestricted nested canonical inputs do not justify that equality. Preserve the intended resource-size result explicitly, or first restrict the corollary to binary lists (τ=B) with one token per entry. Do not silently keep arbitrary τ and merely quote the informal corollary. The binary-list main target Corollary 4.9 does not have this same counterexample.
